Educational Electrical Calculators

Basic browser-based examples for learning formulas. Results are estimates and are not design, installation, protection, or safety recommendations.

Safety notice: Do not use these examples to select cables, breakers, transformers, generators, or make live electrical decisions. Confirm every real design with a qualified professional and applicable standards.

⚡ Motor Load Current — AC / DC · Single & Three Phase

Practice a basic current estimate for a single-phase, three-phase, or DC motor. No cable or protection-device sizing is provided.

🔋 Electric Consumption & Bill Estimation

Kisi bhi load ka daily / monthly / yearly unit (kWh) aur electricity bill estimate karo.

📉 Voltage Drop & Cable Size Check

Practice a simplified resistive voltage-drop estimate. It is not a cable-sizing result.

🔧 Power Factor Improvement & Capacitor Bank

Estimate the ideal reactive-power change between two power-factor values.

🔌 Transformer Load & Turn Ratio

Practice ideal three-phase transformer current, loading, and turns-ratio calculations.

💥 Simplified Transformer Fault Current

Estimate a symmetrical RMS fault-current example using transformer rating and impedance only. Upstream network and other system impedances are not included.

🔄 Motor Synchronous Speed & Slip

Supply frequency aur poles se synchronous speed, aur actual RPM se slip percentage nikaalo.
